# Starvation scenario: one large low-priority task (T1) competes with a steady
# stream of small high-priority tasks (one per tick, 2 ticks each).
# Under Priority, T1 waits until the stream stops. Aging Priority raises T1's
# effective priority (+1 every 3 ticks) until it reaches the head of the queue.
# Strict queue mode is used: in Backfill mode the small tasks keep slipping into
# freed capacity and aging alone cannot rescue T1 (try it to see the limitation).
CONFIG queue_mode strict
CONFIG aging_interval 3
CONFIG starvation_threshold 15
